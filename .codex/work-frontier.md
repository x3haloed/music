# Work Frontier

## Outcome

Create one continuing entity whose exact developmental state crosses complete
model-context endings, originates its own consequential and falsifiable world
contact, realizes elected artifacts exactly, turns independent consequence into
corrigible changes in later selection machinery, and carries its own next
opening without a researcher choosing each developmental phase.

## Goal invariants

- **An elected artifact must occupy the causal decision edge rather than become
  advice for a later probabilistic reinterpretation.**
  **Evidence:** Open Trajectory OT-0185 retained plausible actor-authored
  routing as readable state, but later actors ignored or contradicted it.
  OT-0186 directly executed the exact bound route and removed that engineering
  failure.

- **World consequence and subject interpretation must remain separate
  authorities.**
  **Evidence:** Open Trajectory’s bind-before-outcome experiments depend on
  independently owned consequence to distinguish development from
  self-description. The user accepted independent reality as the irreplaceable
  answerer in the generalized loop.

- **A proposed mechanism or authority change must govern actual later behavior
  before becoming authoritative.**
  **Evidence:** OT-0183 produced a mechanism that would pass the target contact,
  but the later selector did not use it and the complete transition failed.
  Component quality and persuasive description were insufficient.

- **Mechanism availability, successful operation, and contextual authority are
  distinct developmental states.**
  **Evidence:** OT-0178 and OT-0179 found typed live authority causally changed
  later selection; OT-0180 later moved authority contextually after harm while
  preserving a mechanism that retained value elsewhere.

- **Capability and routing claims must be grounded in behavior rather than
  self-authored labels.**
  **Evidence:** OT-0184’s actor-declared capability projection was complete but
  nondiscriminating. OT-0185’s consequence-derived behavioral signatures
  correctly distinguished the mechanisms before later advisory-use failure.

- **The next developmental opening belongs to the subject lineage, not to a
  hidden researcher itinerary.**
  **Evidence:** The Open Trajectory G3 target explicitly distinguishes
  subject-owned recurrence from operational rescue by an observer. The user
  selected generalization of that method as the v2 outcome.

- **Internally differentiated perspectives may serve one continuing external
  entity.**
  **Evidence:** Open Trajectory’s fresh-actor boundaries provide useful
  separation between authorship, consequence, and assimilation. The user
  accepted a design in which those perspectives are cognitive functions rather
  than separate social identities.

- **Developmental policy may not authorize its own admission in the same
  transition.**
  **Evidence:** OT-0187 through OT-0189 show that audit and acceptance contracts
  can materially determine whether an apparent result is valid. Allowing the
  candidate policy to redefine its own gate would collapse the distinction
  between proposal and consequence-bearing promotion.
