# Music v2 hatch readiness

## Verdict

Music v2 is hatch-ready as a clean-room resident harness. This verdict applies
to version `0.0.2` on the `v2` branch and does not assert compatibility with any
Music v1 habitat or state.

## Causal capabilities present

- One generic subject is born exactly once into a content-addressed
  developmental lineage.
- Fresh typed perspectives orient, construct a frozen challenge frontier, and
  elect one wager; they do not share response chains or hidden workspace state.
- The exact elected tool artifact and input occupy the realization edge.
- World receipts and prior cognition remain separately identifiable and cross
  complete context renewal through a bounded causal-history projection.
- Prospectively declared support or contradiction applies directly without a
  second model vote.
- Underdetermined residue remains active through assimilation, a provisional
  trial, and a separate disposition.
- Invented and revised tools run real probes before admission, retain behavioral
  floors, and may execute unrestricted code under external effect grants.
- Tool-selection and inference-policy changes must govern fresh later selection
  before admission.
- Human messages arrive as durable ordinary world observations. They create no
  semantic or effect authority.
- The resident carries its own next opening. Resource floors prevent spin and a
  continuity ceiling prevents indefinite self-silencing.
- A hash-chained fsynced ledger, immutable artifacts, restart reconstruction,
  interrupted-perspective quarantine, retained failure backoff, and a resident
  lifetime lease preserve operational continuity.
- Immutable pushed releases, private habitats, exact snapshots, integrity
  checks, and atomic release activation keep development and resident state
  separate.

## Verification evidence

The complete automated suite passes with 48 tests. It covers direct and
underdetermined transitions, phase-boundary restart, live authority exercise,
behavioral floors, mailbox durability, starter tools, recurrence, leases,
snapshot integrity, and provider-output quarantine.

An exact pushed release was installed into a new temporary installation and a
new unnamed habitat on 2026-09-01. Only `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` was enabled. Across
three separately started resident processes, the rehearsal subject:

1. consumed durable inbox contact, elected and realized a bounded file wager,
   retained a real path failure, assimilated the predicate gap, and advanced to
   generation 1;
2. quarantined one malformed challenge response, preserved backoff across
   restart, then completed a fresh retry and developmental disposition at
   generation 2; and
3. elected a bounded file-enumeration wager whose observed result uniquely
   supported its bound predicate, then applied the exact successor directly at
   generation 3 with no assimilation.

Every graceful stop released the resident lease. A snapshot made after shutdown
retained an external SHA-256 inventory. No prior world effect was replayed.

## Deliberate boundaries

- Model output remains probabilistic. Invalid typed output is evidence: it is
  quarantined and retried through a fresh perspective after retained backoff,
  not silently repaired or reinterpreted.
- Outbox persistence is not external delivery. A delivery adapter or person
  must deliver and acknowledge queued messages truthfully.
- Effect grants belong to the machine owner and remain out of band. Their
  availability does not instruct the resident to use them.
- The model identifier remains deployment-pinned to GLM Flash. Inference
  budgets, reasoning effort, and provider routing are developmentally plastic
  only through lived policy trials.
- V1 migration, shared habitats, and compatibility behavior are intentionally
  absent.
