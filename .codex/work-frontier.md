# Work Frontier

## Outcome

Hatch one exact continuing subject whose content-free recurrence derives the
next warranted developmental operation from its own durable position. Fresh
inference contexts are perspectives of that single subject, not separate
agents. They select, realize, correct, assimilate, or expand only inside the
operation currently warranted by exact state. Deterministic machinery executes
bound contact, owns lifecycle compilation, records consequence, and carries the
subject across model turns and process restarts.

## Goal invariants

- One content-identified subject is the entity. Model calls are fresh
  perspectives of that subject; transcripts and model threads are not its
  continuity.
- An instruction-free pulse supplies time, never a task. Exact developmental
  position mechanically selects `select`, `realize`, `contact`, `correct`,
  `assimilate`, `expand`, or `wait`.
- Operation selection is deterministic and subject-owned. Its prospectively
  installed organ is durable, inspectable, consequence-revisable state.
- Each inference receives one operation-specific projection. It cannot choose
  the phase, restate mechanically settled lifecycle facts, execute sealed
  contact, or admit its own development.
- Active opportunity standing is mechanically derived from retained proposals,
  contacts, consequences, observations, and saturation. Its compact projection
  guides attention but has no target, effect, outcome, or admission authority.
- Selection chooses a developmental stake. Realization separately binds exact
  contact and prospective success, contradiction, and surrender conditions.
  The kernel executes exactly that contact without probabilistic reinterpretation.
- World adapters alone own effect and receipt truth. Descriptive model reports
  never outrank mechanically observed effects or world-owned consequence.
- Operational succession and developmental change are distinct. Tool success,
  model completion, waiting, and phase advancement do not by themselves count
  as learning.
- Correction receives exact contradiction and may revise the failed selection,
  realization, organ, or stake within an explicit mutation surface.
  Assimilation receives exact consequence and must retain, revise, retire, or
  surrender the active stake in structured form.
- Local saturation cannot become subject termination. It routes to expansion;
  exhausted presently reachable contact routes to bounded waiting while later
  contact or a continuity pulse can reopen the position.
- Human messages, failures, filesystem facts, network results, and pulses are
  ordinary world events. Human prose has no special transition authority.
- Every durable organ and tool-facing contract is replaceable through the same
  bind-contact-consequence-assimilate path. The stable core contains only exact
  storage, schema verification, operation derivation, inference invocation,
  world invocation, and successor compilation.
- Exact evidence is content-addressed and append-only. Immediate attention is
  bounded near 200k input tokens without destroying or silently summarizing
  retained evidence.
- Provider/model choice is sealed and explicit. OpenRouter and ephemeral Codex
  CLI contexts remain first-class; prompt caching may reuse prefixes but never
  responses, threads, hidden reasoning, or state authority.
- Local tools remain intentionally unrestricted and unisolated. File reads and
  patches are byte-bounded before allocation; effect retries retain exact
  idempotency identity.
- One resident lease owns advancement. Crash recovery, snapshots, immutable
  installed releases, macOS residence, and the Companion remain operational
  shell, not developmental authorities.
- V3 migration, scientific proof, conversational responsiveness, hostile-code
  containment, distributed residence, and autonomous acquisition of new
  external worlds are outside this clean-room V4 acceptance boundary.

## Evaluation regime

- **Epoch:** V4-HATCH-READY-1
- **Required engineering path:** genesis -> mechanically derived selection ->
  fresh selection judgment -> fresh realization -> deterministic contact -> exact
  consequence classification -> fresh assimilation/correction -> exact successor
  -> restart -> recurrence.
- **Required developmental path:** selection -> contradiction -> correction ->
  prospective organ revision -> later changed selection; and local saturation ->
  expansion or truthful waiting without subject closure.
- **Required operational path:** bounded attention, provider-prefix caching,
  unrestricted local worlds, external observations, grants, residency,
  snapshots, immutable install, Companion inspection, and clean hosted-model
  rehearsal.
- **Claim boundary:** hatch-ready means every claimed causal path exists and is
  verified. It does not mean the resident has already learned wisely or will
  choose a valuable life.

## Prediction errors

- V3 let prose continuation and self-reported scores become a narrowing control
  plane. V4 removes both from operation authority.
- A one-candidate frontier is not meaningful election. V4 separates standing,
  selection, and realization and permits the kernel to request expansion rather
  than rubber-stamp a lone proposal.
- Successful effects are not developmental promotion. V4 maintains separate
  succession and developmental revision counters.
- Opportunity projection can help a fresh perspective see eligible contact, but
  OT-0253 shows it must remain mechanically derived and non-authoritative.
- Exact raw history can exceed a provider ceiling even when entry count is
  bounded. V4 retains evidence by reference and budgets rendered attention.
